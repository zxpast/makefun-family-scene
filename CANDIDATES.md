## C1 — Burnt toast (CHOSEN)

> Dad saves the burnt toast for himself, calls it 'the crispy one,' and every morning Mom pretends to believe him — then eats her own burnt slice too, so he won't eat alone.

- 171 chars / 179 bytes / 33 words. 1 line.
- K1 warm scene: ✓ — a whole breakfast-table household in one breath.
- K2 heartfelt: ✓✓ — Mom eats her own burnt slice so Dad won't eat alone; affection is the
  action, not a sentiment label. Strongest of the five on this criterion.
- K3 funny: ✓ — "the crispy one" + Mom pretending to believe him; light, recognisable.
- K4 economy: ✓ — setup ~26 words, turn ~7 words (ratio ~3.7:1, inside 2:1–5:1); no word wasted.
- K5 specific/fresh: ✓ — one telling detail (burnt toast, "the crispy one"); no greeting-card line.
- Twist in last 8 words: "burnt slice too, so he won't eat alone." ✓

## Decision

**C1 wins.** It is the only candidate strong on all five criteria, and the strongest on K2 —
the criterion this competition's brief is built around ("warm, funny and heartfelt", "genuine
affection rather than sentiment stuck on afterwards"). Its twist is the emotional payoff
itself, in the last words, which is exactly what the skill's pattern (twist last + specific
detail + no explanation) asks for. C4 is the closest alternative; C3 second.
